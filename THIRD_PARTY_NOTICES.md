# Third-party notices

ToolKnit uses only dependencies whose licenses are checked during release preparation.

- Tauri: MIT OR Apache-2.0
- Vue, Pinia, Vue Router, Vite: MIT
- PDF.js: Apache-2.0
- lopdf and printpdf: MIT
- ts-fsrs: MIT
- RapidOCR candidate engine: Apache-2.0 code; model artifacts have separate ownership and are never bundled without a per-artifact license record.
- RapidLaTeXOCR candidate engine: MIT; model artifacts are reviewed separately.

`MODEL_MANIFEST.json` is intentionally absent until a model artifact has an explicit upstream URL, SHA-256, version and redistribution permission.
